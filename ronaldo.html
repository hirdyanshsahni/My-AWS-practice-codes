Cristiano Ronaldo

Cristiano Ronaldo in 2018

Cristiano Ronaldo dos Santos Aveiro is a Portuguese professional footballer who plays as a forward. Widely regarded as one of the greatest football players of all time, he has achieved numerous individual and team honours throughout his career.

Ronaldo began his senior career with Sporting CP before moving to Manchester United in 2003. During his first spell at the club, he established himself as one of the world's leading attacking players and won several major trophies.

In 2009, Ronaldo transferred to Real Madrid, where he became the club's all-time leading goalscorer and won multiple UEFA Champions League titles. He later played for Juventus before returning to Manchester United and subsequently continuing his career with Al-Nassr.

Ronaldo has also represented the Portugal national team since 2003. He has captained Portugal and helped the country win the UEFA European Championship and the UEFA Nations League.

Born: 5 February 1985, Funchal, Madeira, Portugal
Position: Forward
Nationality: Portuguese
Professional debut: 2002

Career

Early career

Ronaldo began playing football at a young age in Madeira. He joined Sporting CP's youth academy and quickly attracted attention because of his technical ability, speed and goal-scoring skills.

He made his first-team debut for Sporting CP in 2002. His performances attracted interest from several European clubs, and in 2003 he joined Manchester United under manager Sir Alex Ferguson.

At Manchester United, Ronaldo developed from a talented young winger into one of the world's most effective attacking players. He won the Premier League, the FA Cup and the UEFA Champions League during his first spell at the club.

Real Madrid

In 2009, Ronaldo joined Real Madrid for a then-world-record transfer fee. He quickly became a central figure in the club's attack and established himself as one of the most prolific goalscorers in football history.

During his time in Madrid, Ronaldo won numerous trophies, including multiple La Liga titles and UEFA Champions League titles. He also won several Ballon d'Or awards during this period.

Ronaldo became Real Madrid's all-time leading goalscorer and scored hundreds of goals for the club across domestic and international competitions.

Juventus

In 2018, Ronaldo moved to Juventus in Italy. He continued to score regularly and helped the club win domestic trophies, while also becoming one of the most recognisable players in Serie A.

His performances in Italy added another major chapter to his career, as he continued to compete at the highest level of European football.

Return to Manchester United

Ronaldo returned to Manchester United in 2021. He scored important goals during his second spell at the club and continued to demonstrate his ability to perform at the highest level.

Al-Nassr

Ronaldo joined Saudi Arabian club Al-Nassr in December 2022. His arrival attracted worldwide attention and contributed to the growing international profile of football in Saudi Arabia.

International career

Ronaldo made his senior debut for Portugal in 2003. He represented Portugal at numerous European Championships and FIFA World Cups, becoming one of the country's most important players.

In 2016, Ronaldo helped Portugal win the UEFA European Championship, the country's first major international football title. He also contributed to Portugal's success in the UEFA Nations League.

Over the course of his international career, Ronaldo became Portugal's record goalscorer and one of the most prolific international goalscorers in men's football.

Achievements

Ronaldo has won numerous individual and team awards during his career. His major achievements include multiple Ballon d'Or awards, UEFA Champions League titles, domestic league titles and international honours with Portugal.

His career has been recognised for exceptional goalscoring, consistency, athleticism and longevity at the highest level of professional football.
